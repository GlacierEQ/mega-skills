# Evidence to Evolution Loop

## Purpose and selection

Use this Combo when repeated task evidence, failures, or quality findings may justify improving a Skill, Combo Skill, Mega Skill, documentation contract, or test. Do **not** use it to self-modify an active route, merge a change, or promote a proposal without review.

## Member contract

| Order | Member | Receives | Must produce | Local validation |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `evidence-register` | Run artifacts, failures, and outcomes | Pattern evidence | Repetition and limitations are traceable. |
| 2 | `claim-calibration` | Claimed improvement and evidence | Scoped rationale | No universal result is inferred from isolated evidence. |
| 3 | `mission-contract` | Improvement opportunity | Change boundary | Owner, impact, non-goals, and acceptance criteria are explicit. |
| 4 | `work-packet` | Approved change boundary | Proposal packet | Required files, checks, and review authority are named. |
| 5 | `quality-gate` | Proposed change and evidence | Evolution gate | Proposal is accepted for review, deferred, or rejected. |

## Handoff contract

Only evidence-backed patterns move to claim calibration. The resulting scoped rationale feeds a bounded improvement contract; the work packet must name any downstream Skill Forge or maintainer review needed. A quality pass admits a proposal for review, not an autonomous repository mutation.

## Compound output

**Artifact:** `CAPABILITY_EVOLUTION_PROPOSAL.md`, including evidence, failure pattern, proposed layer, affected IDs, compatibility risk, non-goals, acceptance criteria, review owner, and first validation.

**Ready for Mega Skill integration when:** the proposal has clear provenance, does not change an active mission contract silently, and can be routed through Skill Forge or the appropriate governed change process.
