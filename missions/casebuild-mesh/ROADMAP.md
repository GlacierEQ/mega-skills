# ROADMAP — Casebuild Mesh

1. **Activation** — establish durable state and register the new Skill/Mega Skill.
2. **Operational-home integration** — add the casebuild framework to the DOCKETS case workspace through its safe branch/PR route.
3. **Identity and integrity** — reconcile stable evidence IDs, source locators, SHA-256 records, derivatives, and exceptions.
4. **Navigation** — connect chronology, docket, people/entities, witnesses, discovery, exhibits, and attorney review.
5. **Cross-system reconciliation** — detect orphans, broken links, stale states, duplicates, and conflicts across peer nodes.
6. **Review and completion** — route consequential decisions, score workstreams, and maintain an exact continuation frontier.

Each phase emits evidence and updates `HANDOFF.md`; no phase silently erases earlier source or analysis state.
