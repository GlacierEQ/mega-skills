# Precision Provider Email

## Purpose

Make each outgoing email factually accurate, easy to process and durable as evidence without making avoidable admissions or unsupported accusations.

## Trigger and preconditions

Use when its contract is needed in a recovered and correctly scoped mission. Source-native evidence, current consent, provider identity and the already-existing work graph outrank assumptions. This Skill does not grant external action authorization.

## Execution method

1. Obtain prior full thread, known record IDs, exact authority, current position, intended authorized sender, governing process and whether an email is legally sufficient for the action.
2. Write one clear subject: exact case/reference + discrete request; appropriate first-person name and capacity. Open with what was sent/received and documented dates; distinguish allegation, claimant observation and independent fact.
3. Ask numbered, measurable questions or record categories addressed to the actual custodian. Seek staged/segregable production, native versions, retention/preservation confirmation, index of withholdings, custodian referral and method for secure identity verification when appropriate.
4. Cite only legal rules validated by Jurisdiction Authority Citation Check; refer to the recipient's written stated procedure, response expectation or review route accurately. Do not invent a deadline, assert the agency has denied when it sought verification, or overstate rights.
5. Specify preferred electronic delivery and ask for decision in writing, categorical disposition, identified redaction authority and next procedural step; reserve applicable rights in neutral terms rather than performative threats.
6. Adversarial final review: false facts, stale status, adverse admissions, privacy leakage, jurisdiction mismatch, identity mismatch, repeated demand, unsupported sanction claim, procedural route and unnecessary emotional language.
7. Produce draft plus verified attachments/recipient manifest; require explicit approval before sending externally and provider SENT/readback afterward.

## Negative and regression tests

- Reject: Same demand resubmitted despite today's send
- Reject: Reply from third party as first-party without authorization
- Reject: Unsupported mandatory deadline
- Reject: Copying IG controlled document to broad CC

## Output and acceptance

EMAIL_DRAFT.md, RECIPIENT_MANIFEST.json and PRE_SEND_GATE.md with line-by-line claim/source/authority linkage.

Acceptance requires the named output fields, exact source/provider IDs or explicit unknowns, a negative-path check and a reproducible next action. No mission completion or provider result may be inferred merely from this document.
