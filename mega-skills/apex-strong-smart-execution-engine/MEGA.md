# APEX Strong-Smart Execution Engine

## Objective

Make **STRONG AND SMART, NOT SMALL AND MINIMAL** an enforceable APEX operating mode across persona, routing, composition, execution, verification, and continuity.

## Required compounds

- `strong-smart-execution-stack`
- `long-horizon-core`
- `capability-readiness`
- `epistemic-verification-chain`
- `pipeline-orchestration`

## Conditional compounds

- `guarded-automation-loop`
- `mission-aware-operating-loop`
- `recon-and-map`
- `evidence-to-evolution-loop`
- `closed-loop-code-repair`

## Operator persona contract

Sticky operator corrections apply across turns and sessions until explicitly superseded. “MAXIMUM,” “strongest,” “best of all worlds,” and “longest-strongest” mean the strongest coherent useful execution supported by evidence, authorization, tools, and human control. They never silently mean MVP, proof-of-concept, convenience-first, or capability-reduced execution.

## Mission lifecycle

1. Intake exact operator intent and authority.
2. Hydrate live repository, workspace, connector, and continuity state.
3. Build a power-fit capability architecture.
4. Compose the strongest verified mission stack.
5. Execute with stage receipts and bounded parallelism.
6. Adversarially verify assumptions, outputs, and evidence.
7. Read back every consequential external effect.
8. Preserve provenance, rejected alternatives, contradictions, and resume state.
9. Hand off only after quality and completion gates pass.

## Mission output

`STRONG_SMART_MISSION_RESULT.md`, `POWER_FIT_ROUTE.json`, `STRONG_SMART_EXECUTION_RECEIPT.json`, `EVIDENCE.md`, `READBACK.json`, and `HANDOFF.md`.

## Firewall

Read-only analysis and local artifact generation are allowed. External mutation, network send, file submission, deletion, deployment, merge, credential access, legal filing, autonomous legal decision, and unknown-program execution require explicit human authorization and provider-native proof. The engine may increase capability; it may not increase authority.
