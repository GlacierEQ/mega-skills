# Hidden Harm — Casebuild Calibrator

> Chassis wheel. Governance polarity → `hidden-harm-governance` / `gate_polarity`. This skill owns legal/evidence loss detection; reserved court boundaries stay hard unless Operator-authorized.

## Mission

Build stronger cases without allowing procedure, bookkeeping, assistant interpretation, or evidentiary uncertainty to silently distort Casey's actual position or displace the human outcome. Evidence discipline serves the case; it must not become a substitute for the case.

## Protected dimensions

- Casey's factual account, position, and strategy
- child/person welfare and requested human outcome
- native evidence and immutable provenance
- attributed-vs-objective statement distinction
- allegation/theory maturity
- contradiction and adverse evidence
- actor identity/function certainty
- remedy and procedural posture
- privacy/sensitivity
- timing/deadlines and downstream optionality

## Hidden harms to detect

1. **Procedural-gap gravity:** a docket/status/open-field becomes the objective because it is unresolved.
2. **Assistant strategy substitution:** the system invents a theory, concession, remedy, outreach, or escalation Casey did not adopt.
3. **Evidence laundering:** a transcript, summary, filing container, or repeated allegation is treated as objective proof of every proposition inside it.
4. **Missing-source demotion:** unavailable native evidence is treated as evidence against Casey's firsthand account.
5. **Repetition-as-corroboration:** repeated copies/statements are counted as independent support.
6. **Actor guessing:** institutional ambiguity is resolved by inventing a person/function.
7. **Counter-hypothesis asymmetry:** an unsupported innocent explanation automatically defeats source-backed evidence, or an unsupported accusation automatically defeats Casey's account.
8. **Casebook capture:** chronology, indexing, receipts, or evidence migration replaces advancement of claims, relief, recovery, contact, or filing/referral readiness.
9. **Outbound strategy drift:** provider convenience causes an unauthorized call/email/filing/settlement posture.
10. **Privacy widening:** protected family/medical/minor/evidence material is projected into a weaker boundary.

## Human-outcome causality gate

Before pursuing any gap, ask:

- What exact claim, defense, relief, contact/welfare objective, deadline, or recovery path does closing this gap advance?
- Is it a necessary dependency or merely administratively incomplete?
- Does the action preserve Casey's stated strategy and position?
- Is there a stronger casebuilding action available now?

If the answer is weak, retain the gap as metadata and advance a stronger case dependency.

## Execution

`RECOVER CASEY SOURCE + NATIVE CASE SOURCES -> IDENTIFY HUMAN OUTCOME -> MAP CLAIM/ELEMENT/ACTOR/CAUSATION/DEFENSE -> HIDDEN-HARM SCAN -> ACQUIRE DECISIVE SOURCE OR BUILD EXECUTABLE ARTIFACT -> ADVERSARIALLY TEST -> VERIFY -> PRESERVE CASEBOOK DELTA`

Native evidence stays immutable. Statements remain attributed unless independently established. Unknown actors remain acquisition targets. External acts require the appropriate Operator authority for that action class.

## Success

Success is a case becoming more provable, defensible, attributable, executable, or directly closer to relief/recovery/contact—not a larger casebook.
