## Ceiling law

Overgovernance is out. Bounded-minimum, canonical-governor headers, and "just enough to keep the operator going" are not design.

- **Human first.** Copy and hierarchy a recruiter can feel in three seconds.
- **Match the house.** Casey's live voice is already on `/` — *I make powerful AI dependable enough to use.* New pages join that, they do not invent a second brand.
- **One signature.** Spend boldness once. Quiet around it.
- **Proof in the browser.** 200 is not enough. Click the path a human uses. Desktop and phone.
- **Ship the door.** If recruiters type `/hire`, that URL exists and is excellent.

Static `site-v15` is **existing-codebase + expressive** (portfolio). Do not reach for React waterfalls on a static HTML plane.
